GiftLoop

The perfect gift idea hits you in March, in passing, and by December you're panic-buying candles again. GiftLoop catches ideas when they happen and remembers what you already gave.

Catch an idea

Add your people and their birthdays. Save gift ideas the moment they occur, rate them, and the best float to the top. Log every gift you give - the repeat-risk radar flags any new idea that looks too much like something they already unwrapped. Everything stays in your browser.
